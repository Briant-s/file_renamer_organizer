| No | Questions | Descriptions |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | Describe the idea / product in a few sentences? | Briefly explain what this idea/product should do and how it should be used. Provide as much detail as you can about it |
| 2 | What problems will this idea/product solve? | Describe the users’ issue this idea/product will be able to help them with. Also, write down if anyone has already managed to successfully solve that issue and what makes your idea different/better from theirs |
| 3 | What are my goals with this idea/product? | Write down all available details regarding your financial, customer-related and operational goals that are tied in to this idea/product |
| 4 | What research have I already done? | If you have conducted any kind of research in regards to this idea/product, issue it’s trying to solve or target audience, write down the results you gathered, as well as any and all insights you’ve acquired during the process |
| 5 | Is there any existing research data already available? | If there’s available data, write down all the information you can find (links, articles, summaries, papers…) about conducted surveys, market research, analytics, customer support or any other conducted research that could be in any way connected to this idea/product |
| 6 | Is there anything I already learned from the feedback? | Write down any and all insights you already got from the type of conducted research or conclusions you were able to draw from them |
| 7 | Based on my idea/product, what 3 aspects would I single out as the most important? | It could be anything from features to visual elements, communication, technology that should be used or anything else that you believe is crucially important for the success of this idea/product |
| 8 | Based on my idea/product, what 3 aspects would I single out as the least important? | Same description as in the previous question, just flipped. Write down aspects you believe are not important for the success of this idea/product |
| 9 | Why is my idea/product different from others on the market? | Briefly explain why your/idea product is or should be different from others that try to solve similar issues, or other ideas/product on the market in general. Write down and explain all elements that make your idea stand out |
| 10 | What are my long term goals with this idea/product | Define and write down your 1 year, 3 year, 5 year, and 10 year goals, as well as end goals you’re trying to achieve with this idea/product |
