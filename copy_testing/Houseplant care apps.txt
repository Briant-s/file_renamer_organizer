App Idea Brainstorming: "PlantPal"

Concept: An app that helps people take care of their houseplants.

Features:
- Image recognition to identify the plant species.
- Watering schedule push notifications.
- "Sickness" identifier (users upload a photo of yellowing leaves, app suggests what's wrong).
- Community forum to trade cuttings.

Monetization:
- Freemium model (basic watering reminders free, sickness identifier is $2.99/mo).
- Ads for local nurseries.

Competitors:
- Planta
- PictureThis
Note: We need a better UI than competitors. Keep it minimal and green.
